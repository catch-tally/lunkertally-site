The quickest way to reach us is right in the app: Account Settings, Help, Send Feedback. It goes straight to the team.

Or email us at hello@lunkertally.com.

Delete your account

  1. Open the app and go to Account Settings, then Account.
  2. Tap Delete Account and follow the steps.

It deletes your account and everything in it right away: your catches, spots, photos, trips, notes, XP, badges and settings. Messages you sent in comp chats are wiped. More in the Privacy Policy.

Can't get into the app? Email hello@lunkertally.com from the address on your account and we'll delete it within 30 days.
