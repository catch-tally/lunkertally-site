Privacy Policy

LunkerTally. Version 2.2. Last updated November 19, 2026.

LunkerTally is a fishing app made in Canada. This page says what we collect, why, who can see it, and what you can do about it.

The short version: your fishing log is yours. Your exact spots are never shown to anyone else. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you around the internet.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address, a password (our sign-in provider keeps it scrambled, so even we can't read it) and the username you pick. When you change your username, the old one is held for 31 days so nobody else can take it while you might switch back.
  • Your settings: where you fish (country and province or state), units, temperature, your tally buttons and the other choices in Account Settings. Also your phone's time zone, so your daily streak and weekly challenges follow your own day.
  • Your fishing log: catches (species, length, weight, time, lure or bait, notes, released or kept, logged live or later) and trips (title, water, times, weather, water temperature, the fish you picked for the trip, notes).
  • Journal notes: notes you write in your Journal, with their day and any lake, town or trip you add. They're private to you, like your catches.
  • Photos you add to catches. Your phone shrinks them and strips their hidden details, like where the photo was taken, before they upload. We also save whether a photo came from the camera or your photo library, when it was taken, and a fingerprint of the picture, so comps can spot a copied photo.
  • Your profile photo, if you add one. It's shrunk to 256 pixels and saved again on your phone first, which drops hidden details like where it was taken. It's kept private and shown only where people already see your name: to members of comps you're in (leaderboards, member lists and chat). You can remove it any time. Comp members can report a photo to our team, who can hide it.
  • Spots: only if you turn on Save My Spots or drop a pin, the exact place of that catch, kept with your account. It's also how a catch lands in its What's Biting area (see below).
  • Areas: with Save My Spots off, a catch you log right then keeps only which 25 km What's Biting area it was in, never the spot, kept with your account, and only when the app already has your OK to use location and Include my catches in What's Biting is on.
  • Weather on your catches: the temperature, wind, pressure, cloud, rain, moon and daylight when each catch was caught. Our server looks it up from the catch's time and its spot or area. For a catch with neither, your phone sends the lake or town you picked in Where You Fish, rounded to about a kilometre, for that one lookup. Only the weather is kept, not the place.
  • Comps and chat: comps you make or join, messages you send, flags, the calls a comp's owner or Judges make on catches, people you block, and the messages and profile photos you report. If our team hides something or mutes someone, we keep a note of that.
  • Feedback: what you write, the screen you were on, the time, the app version, and your phone's model and system version. A picture of the app's own screen, never anything else on your phone, only if you turn that on.
  • XP, levels, streaks, badges and weekly challenges, worked out from all of the above.
  • Your agreement: which version of the Terms of Use and this Privacy Policy you agreed to, and when.

What stays on your phone

Some things never leave your phone: the location used to find your What's Open zone, what you're Throwing, your Save My Spots choice, your language, your reminder settings, and the fish you picked for Early Bird.

Your What's Open zone and the lake or town you picked in Where You Fish are kept on your phone, not with your account. When the Forecast or a catch's weather uses that lake or town, the app sends only its location, rounded to about a kilometre (2 decimals), for that one answer.

With Save My Spots on, the Forecast uses where you are, rounded the same way. If you tap Use My Location on the Forecast instead, that rounded spot is kept on your phone for next time. Our server uses it for that one answer and doesn't keep it with your account.

What's Biting only ever sends the id of a 25 km area, your zone's number, your Great Lake if you're on one, and the day, never a spot. The area comes from the lake or town you picked in Where You Fish, or, when the app already has your OK to use location, from where you are, worked out on your phone. Our server uses it for that one answer and doesn't keep it.

Streak reminders and Early Bird alerts are set up on your phone itself. No notification server gets involved.

Crash reports

When the app crashes, it sends our team a short crash report so we can fix the bug: what went wrong and where in the app, the app version, and your phone's model and system version. No account or catch data goes with it, and nothing that says who you are or where you fish. It reaches the team's private Discord channel, and our server deletes its copy after 7 days. So one phone can't send too many, the app also sends a random number it made on your phone, not tied to your account.

If you've allowed it in your phone's settings, Apple or Google may also share crash reports with us through their developer tools. Those come from them, under their own privacy policies, and they're about the app (like the phone model, system version and where in the app it broke), not your log.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your data, ever.
  • No ads, and no ad or tracking tools in the app.
  • We don't share your data with anyone except the services that run the app, listed below.

Where it's kept

Your account, log and photos are kept with Supabase, the service that runs our database, sign-in and photo storage, in their Canada (Central) region in Montreal. Everything travels encrypted.

Feedback, chat reports and profile photo reports (with a link to the photo that works for 7 days) are also posted to the team's private Discord channel, so a real person sees them fast. Discord is a company in the United States.

Drop a Pin shows a map from Apple on iPhone or Google on Android. The map company gets the usual requests a map makes, never your log.

Weather comes from MET Norway (the Norwegian Meteorological Institute). Only our server asks them, never the app, and it sends a location rounded to about a kilometre (2 decimals), with nothing that says who you are. Our server keeps their answers for that rounded spot for a while, so anglers nearby share them. No user is saved with them.

Gusts, the chance of rain and weather alerts come from Environment and Climate Change Canada. Only our server asks them, never the app, and it sends a location rounded to about a kilometre, or the id of the nearest city forecast, with nothing that says who you are. Our server keeps their answers for a short while (alerts 10 minutes, forecasts 30), with no user saved with them.

Great Lakes water temperatures come from NOAA's Great Lakes Environmental Research Laboratory in the United States. Our server asks once a day for the lake-wide averages. Nothing about you or your location goes with it.

Who can see what

  • Only you: your whole log, your exact spots, your notes and Journal notes, the weather on your catches, your trips, your settings, your feedback and your agreement record.
  • People in a comp with you: your username and profile photo (if you add one), a Pro badge if you have Pro, and the catches that count in that comp (species, size, weight, time, photo, and whether it was logged later). They see your messages in that comp's chat. They never see your spots, your notes, or any catch outside the comp.
  • A comp's owner and Judges: if one of your catches in that comp gets held or flagged, they see it in Needs Review and approve or reject it (when it's the owner's own catch, a Judge or another member does). Their calls show to the comp. Nobody in a comp sees who flagged a catch.
  • Everyone else: no profile and nothing with your name on it. If you leave What's Biting on, your catches with a spot or an area can count toward the totals for their area, with no name and no spot (see What's Biting).
  • The team: what we need to run the app, fix problems and handle reports. A report includes a copy of the message.

Your exact spots are never shown to anyone else. Not to comp mates, not in What's Biting, not anywhere.

What's Biting

What's Biting shows which fish are being caught near you, and on what, from catches anglers logged with a spot or an area. It's all worked out on our server, and your phone only ever gets totals. Here's how it keeps spots and names out of it:

  • It works in fixed areas, a grid of squares about 25 km across. It never draws a circle around anyone, so moving around can't narrow down where someone else fished.
  • It only shows an area once at least 3 different anglers, not counting whoever is looking, have logged there, and it always says how many catches and anglers it's based on.
  • It never shows an exact spot, a name, or who caught what. Only totals for the area: which fish, how many, the top lures and the usual time of day. A lure someone typed in themselves shows only as "Other lure".
  • To keep it honest, each angler counts for at most 10 catches a day, and custom fish, flagged comp catches and any week of an angler's log that Smells Fishy? flags are left out. Fish that are closed in your zone that day are left out too.
  • Catches with no spot and no area are never used.
  • It only uses catches from anglers who leave Include my catches in What's Biting on. It's on to start, and you can turn it off anytime in Account Settings, Privacy. Your catches drop out the next time anyone checks.

Deleting your account

Delete Account in Account Settings deletes your account and everything in it right away: your catches, spots, photos, profile photo, trips, notes, Journal notes, feedback, XP, badges, settings and agreement record.

Messages you sent in comp chats are wiped too. Each one keeps its place in the chat as "Deleted Angler: message removed", so the conversation still reads in order for everyone else. A comp you made with other anglers in it goes to whoever joined first.

If someone reported one of your messages, the team keeps the copy in that report, with no name on it, to finish handling the report.

Copies of your feedback or reports already posted to the team's Discord channel get removed when you ask our Privacy Officer. Our hosting provider's backups can hold a copy for a short time after that, then it's gone for good.

Downloading your data

Download My Data in Account Settings gives you one zip file: a spreadsheet (CSV, it opens in any spreadsheet app) with your account details, every trip and every catch, spots, notes and weather included, and a folder with every photo you added. Your Journal notes, profile photo and agreement record are in it too. Save it or send it wherever you like.

If a photo is missing from it, ask our Privacy Officer and we'll send you a copy of your photos.

Your rights in Canada

We follow Canada's privacy law for businesses, the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), and Quebec's privacy law if you live there. You can:

  • See the personal information we have about you and get a copy. Download My Data covers most of it.
  • Fix anything that's wrong. You can change most of it yourself in the app.
  • Take back your OK for something, like turning off What's Biting or Save My Spots, or delete your account.
  • Ask us questions or make a complaint. Our Privacy Officer answers every privacy request within 30 days.

If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca.

Kids

The app is for people 13 and older. It isn't for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child under 13 has an account, tell us and we'll delete it.

Keeping it safe

Access rules on our database mean each person can only reach their own data, plus what a comp shares. Photos are kept private and shown with links that stop working after a short time. No system is perfect, so we only keep what the app needs.

Changes

Each version has a number and a date at the top. A small fix just updates them. If it's a big change, we'll tell you in the app first and ask you to accept it.

Contact

Questions about your privacy, or a request about your information? Write to our Privacy Officer at hello@lunkertally.com, or use Contact Us in the app's Account Settings.
