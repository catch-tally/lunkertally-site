Safety Tips

LunkerTally. Version 1.1. Last updated November 19, 2026.

A few habits that keep a good day on the water a good day.

Check the rules before you keep a fish

What's Open is a quick guide. Ontario's official rules are the final word, and they can change during the year.

  • Ontario Fishing Regulations Summary

Watch the weather and the wind

Wind and waves can build fast on big water like the Great Lakes. Check before you go and keep an eye on the sky. If it's getting rough, head in early.

Wear a life jacket

Even if you swim well. It only works if you have it on.

Tell someone where you are going

Let someone know where you'll be fishing and when you'll be back.
