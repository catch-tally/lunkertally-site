LunkerTally. Version 2.1. Last updated November 19, 2026.

These are the rules for using LunkerTally. Making an account means you agree to them, and to our Community Rules, which are part of these terms. They're in plain words, but they still count.

Who can use it

  • You're 13 or older. If you're under the age of majority where you live, you have a parent or guardian's OK.
  • One account per person. Keep your password to yourself.
  • Use a real email address you can get to.

The app is a helper

Everything in the app is general information, provided as is: What's Open, the Forecast, What's Biting, Smells Fishy?, the fish guide and the rest. It comes from public sources and from other anglers, and it can be wrong, missing something or out of date.

What's Open is a helper, not the law. Fishing rules can change during the year, and the official Ontario Fishing Regulations and Ontario's variation orders are what count.

Weather can change faster than any forecast. The Forecast is for planning, not for safety decisions, and Environment Canada's alerts come straight from them.

You're responsible

  • Fishing legally: your licence, seasons, limits, sizes, sanctuaries and any other rule where you fish.
  • Your own safety and the safety of anyone with you. Check the weather, the water and the ice yourself, and wear a life jacket.
  • Your decisions: where and when to fish, what to keep, and whether to go out at all. The app can help you plan, but those calls are yours.
  • Using the app safely: don't use it while you're driving a boat or a car.

Your stuff

You own your catches, photos, notes and messages. You let us store them, copy them as the app needs (like making smaller versions of photos), and show them the way the app works: to you, and to people in comps with you, as the Privacy Policy explains.

Photos in comps: when you're in a comp, you give us a free, non-exclusive licence to show your catch photos that count there, and your profile photo, to that comp's members, with the catch and your username. It lasts while the photo is in the app, and ends when you remove the photo or the catch, or delete your account. We don't use your photos for ads and we don't sell them.

Only post photos and words you have the right to share.

Community Rules and content

There's zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Follow the Community Rules everywhere in the app: chats, comp names, rewards, usernames, profile photos, catches and photos. In short, don't post:

  • Hate, slurs, or attacks on anyone for who they are
  • Harassment, bullying or threats
  • Sexual content
  • Fake catches, or someone else's photo
  • Other people's spots or personal information
  • Illegal fishing, or anything else illegal
  • Spam, scams or ads
  • Pretending to be someone else

A word filter blocks the worst words, and anyone can Report a message or a photo, or Block someone. We aim to look at every report within 24 hours. We can remove or hide any content, reject catches, mute someone from every chat, or suspend or close an account that breaks these terms, with or without warning.

Fair play

Comps are for fun between friends, so log real fish. Flags, the calls of a comp's owner and Judges, and Smells Fishy? help keep it honest, and the owner and Judges have the last word on that comp's catches. We can remove catches or accounts that cheat.

Rewards are between the people in the comp. We don't hold, pay or promise any reward, and comps can't have entry fees or betting.
