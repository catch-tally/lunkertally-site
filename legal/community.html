Community Rules

LunkerTally. Version 1.2. Last updated November 19, 2026.

LunkerTally is for anglers who like a little friendly competition. Keep it fun for everyone. These rules are part of the Terms of Use.

Be decent

  • No harassment, bullying or threats. Trash talk about fish is fine, going after a person is not.
  • No hate, slurs, or attacks on anyone for who they are.
  • No sexual content.

Log real fish

  • No fake catches or made up sizes.
  • Only your own photos of your own fish. No pictures from the internet or a buddy's phone.
  • In a comp, only fish caught during the comp. Don't pass off an old fish as a new one.

Keep other people private

  • Don't share someone else's fishing spots without their OK.
  • Don't share anyone's personal information: their real name, address, phone number, where they live or work, or photos of them.

Fish legally

  • Don't post illegal fishing, like fish kept out of season, over the limit or under size, or fish from a sanctuary.
  • Don't use the app to plan breaking fishing laws.

No spam

  • No spam, scams or ads.
  • Don't pretend to be someone else.

What happens

Report a message or a photo, or Block someone, and the team takes a look. We can hide content, reject catches, mute someone from every chat, or close an account.
